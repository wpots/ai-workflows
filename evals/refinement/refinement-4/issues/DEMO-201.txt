# Wishlist page for logged-in customers

⏱️  Wed, 28 Oct 26  🔎 Demo Shop PO  🚀 Medium  📦 None  🏷️  None

------------------------ Description ------------------------

## Description
Logged-in customers save products with the heart on a product card and find them back on a wishlist page in their account.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A logged-in customer adds a product to the wishlist with the heart on its product card.
- [ ] The wishlist page in the account lists the saved products with image, name and price.
- [ ] A customer removes a product from the wishlist page.
- [ ] A visitor who is not logged in is asked to log in after tapping the heart.
